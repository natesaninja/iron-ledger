      Coverage vs MED

      Why MED volume

      Bars show planned hard-set equivalents vs a minimum effective weekly target for the month. Aim for ~70%+ on primaries — that is enough to progress when time is scarce. Chasing 150% on everything usually steals recovery.

      MED principles

      Recovery

      Hard sets create fatigue that decays over ~2–3 days (legs longer). Post-nights: mark rest, not iron. Remove a train day and rebuild — coverage rebalances so effort isn’t wasted on the wrong muscles.

      MED supplements

      Only what earns its place. Educational — not medical advice. Food, sleep, and the workout plan do the heavy lifting; pills don’t fix a missed session.

      What we skip on purpose

      BCAAs (if protein is already enough), fat burners, “test boosters,” and most pre-workout kitchen sinks. Under time pressure those rarely beat creatine + protein + optional caffeine + fixing real deficiencies.
