      Why MED volume

      Planned bars show what the planner assigned. Logged bars show hard sets you actually entered on Today. Aim for ~70%+ on primaries. Chasing 150% on everything usually steals recovery.

      MED principles

      Recovery

      Hard sets create fatigue that decays over ~2–3 days (legs longer). Post-nights: mark rest, not iron. Remove a train day and rebuild — coverage rebalances so effort isn’t wasted on the wrong muscles.

      Training evidence

      Search training supplements by name or claim. See evidence grades, studied dose, timing, and cautions. Educational — not medical advice. Food, sleep, and the plan do the heavy lifting.

      How to read grades

      Strong / moderate = human research mostly supports the claim for training contexts. Mixed = depends or inconsistent. Weak / insufficient = don’t build your stack on it. Dose lines are studied / practical ranges, not personalized prescriptions.

      What we skip on purpose

      Fat burners, “test boosters,” and most pre-workout kitchen sinks. Under time pressure those rarely beat creatine + protein + optional caffeine + fixing real deficiencies. BCAAs and HMB are listed so you can see why they’re low priority.
