# Iron Ledger — easy sharing (private pilot)

## One-tap invite links (best)

Send **only the link** — no separate code. They open it and the app unlocks.

| Who | Link |
|-----|------|
| **You** | https://natesaninja.github.io/iron-ledger/?i=pilot |
| **Coworker 1** | https://natesaninja.github.io/iron-ledger/?i=crew1 |
| **Coworker 2** | https://natesaninja.github.io/iron-ledger/?i=crew2 |
| **Coworker 3** | https://natesaninja.github.io/iron-ledger/?i=crew3 |

### How to share from the app

1. Unlock Iron Ledger yourself (`?i=pilot` or code below).  
2. **Settings → Share (one tap)**  
3. **Copy link** or **Share** next to Coworker 1 / 2 / 3  
4. Text them that message  

They: open link in **Safari** → app opens → Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
